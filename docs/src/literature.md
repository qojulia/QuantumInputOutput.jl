# Relevant Literature

QuantumInputOutput.jl follows the virtual-cavity input-output construction introduced by Kiilerich and Mølmer [Kiilerich2019](@cite) and developed into a general pulse formalism in [Kiilerich2020](@cite).

Its network composition layer is based on the SLH framework reviewed by Combes, Kerckhoff, and Sarovar [Combes2017](@cite). The interaction-picture treatment of propagating quantum pulses follows Christiansen, Kiilerich, and Mølmer [Christiansen2023](@cite), while the virtual-cavity treatment of finite propagation delays follows Christiansen and Mølmer [Christiansen2026](@cite).
